Our History

Back in 2015, during the Milan Expo, something happened that I'll never forget. A close family friend came to visit, and I found myself helping the first girl I ever loved choose a dress. Her eyes lit up with excitement, but her family decided not to buy it.

I saw how sad she was, and even though I was only 12, it left a deep mark on me. In that moment, I promised myself that one day I would create a fashion label where no one I love would have to hear "no" when feeling special in a design you chose — while you see the love of your life smile.

With passion and hard work, Voulez-Vous was born — built on love, loyalty, and the dream of creating something meaningful to connect people through the power of fashion. Stay with us as we grow and win together.

— Voulez-Vous, Born To Win
